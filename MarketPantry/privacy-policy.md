# MarketPantry Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026

MarketPantry is an unofficial iOS client for browsing and participating in PTT. It is not operated, sponsored, endorsed, or owned by PTT. PTT names, content, and services belong to and are operated by their respective owners. MarketPantry retrieves PTT data through PTT's public web pages and terminal service, then presents that data in a redesigned interface.

This policy explains what information the app stores, what third-party services it contacts, and what the app does not collect.

## Information Stored on Your Device

MarketPantry may store the following information locally on your device:

- App settings, notification preferences, blacklists, whitelists, drafts, and saved image URL metadata
- Your PTT account name and, if you choose to save it, your PTT password
- Optional image-host credentials that you enter
- Market sentiment records derived from public PTT content, retained for up to 48 hours

Passwords and image-host credentials are stored using Apple Keychain. Preferences and lists are stored using Apple UserDefaults. Market sentiment files are stored in the app's Application Support directory with iOS data protection. Author identifiers in those sentiment records are salted and hashed.

## PTT Account and Content

When you sign in, your credentials are sent directly from the app to PTT for authentication. MarketPantry does not operate an account server or proxy that receives your PTT password.

PTT handles the account, articles, push comments, replies, and other content that you read or submit. That activity is subject to PTT's own rules, moderation, availability, and data practices.

## Third-Party Services

MarketPantry contacts third-party services only to provide app functionality:

- PTT: sign-in, public boards and articles, push comments, posts, replies, and related account operations
- ImgBB, Imgur, or Catbox: optional image upload after you explicitly select an image
- External websites: links that you choose to open from PTT content

When the app contacts these services, standard network information such as your IP address may be processed by those services as part of normal internet communication. Content submitted to PTT or uploaded to an image host is handled according to that service's own policies.

## Photos and On-Device Analysis

MarketPantry accesses a photo only after you choose it for an app feature such as image upload or a saved-image action. Selected images may be sent to the image host you choose.

Saved-image text recognition and classification use Apple's Vision framework on your device. MarketPantry does not send those analysis results to its own server.

## User-Generated Content and Local Filtering

PTT contains user-generated content supplied by PTT users. PTT, not MarketPantry, operates and moderates that platform.

MarketPantry's blacklist and whitelist features only change how content is presented on your device. They do not delete, report, edit, or otherwise alter content on PTT. Objectionable or unlawful content should be handled through PTT's own rules and reporting or moderation mechanisms where available.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, MarketPantry may schedule local notifications for app features such as market sentiment changes or selected user activity. Notification preferences are stored on your device. The app does not operate a remote push-notification server.

## What MarketPantry Does Not Do

MarketPantry does not:

- Sell personal data
- Use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs
- Track you across apps or websites
- Operate its own server for collecting PTT credentials or user content
- Access your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location
- Change or remove content from PTT through its local blacklist or whitelist

## App Store Privacy Disclosure

MarketPantry's privacy manifest and App Store disclosures may identify User ID, Other User Content, and Photos or Videos as data used for app functionality. These categories cover account operations, content you choose to submit to PTT, and images you choose to upload. The app does not use these categories for tracking or advertising.

Data processed only on your device, such as local Vision analysis, is not collected by MarketPantry.

# MarketPantry 隱私權政策摘要

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 15 日

MarketPantry 是用來瀏覽與參與 PTT 的非官方 iOS 用戶端，並非由 PTT 營運、贊助、背書或持有。PTT 的名稱、內容與服務屬於並由其各自權利人營運。MarketPantry 透過 PTT 公開網頁與終端服務取得資料，再以重新設計的介面呈現。

App 可能在裝置本機保存設定、通知偏好、黑白名單、草稿、圖片網址、PTT 帳號名稱、你選擇保存的密碼，以及圖片託管服務憑證。密碼與憑證使用 Apple Keychain 儲存。市場情緒資料由公開 PTT 內容在本機產生，最多保留 48 小時，其中作者 ID 會經過加鹽雜湊。

登入時，帳號密碼會由 App 直接傳送給 PTT 驗證。MarketPantry 沒有接收 PTT 密碼或使用者內容的自有後端伺服器。你在 PTT 閱讀或提交的文章、推文與回覆，由 PTT 處理，並受 PTT 自身規則、審核與資料政策約束。

只有當你主動選擇圖片時，App 才會將圖片上傳至你選擇的 ImgBB、Imgur 或 Catbox。圖片文字辨識與分類使用 Apple Vision 在裝置上執行，分析結果不會傳送至 MarketPantry 自有伺服器。

PTT 包含 PTT 使用者產生的內容，平台由 PTT 營運與審核。MarketPantry 的黑名單與白名單只改變內容在你裝置上的呈現，不會刪除、檢舉、修改或影響 PTT 上的內容。不當或違法內容應依 PTT 自身規則與其提供的處理機制處理。

MarketPantry 不使用廣告或第三方分析 SDK、不進行跨 App 或網站追蹤、不出售個人資料，也不會存取聯絡人、麥克風、相機或精確位置。
