# TuneNow Support

cocoaswifty@gmail.com

## Common Questions

### Why does a station fail to play?

TuneNow uses public radio station data and third-party streaming URLs. Some stations may be offline, restricted by region, moved to a new stream URL, or temporarily unavailable.

Try another station or try again later.

### Why is the current song not shown?

Current song information depends on whether the radio station provides stream metadata. Not every station provides this information.

### Why is album artwork missing or incorrect?

TuneNow uses Apple iTunes Search to look up album artwork from the current song title. Artwork may be missing or incorrect when the station metadata is incomplete, formatted differently, or not matched by search.

### Why are some countries or categories missing stations?

Station availability depends on the Radio Browser data source and each station's stream status. Categories without playable stations may be hidden in the app.
