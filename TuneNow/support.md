# TuneNow Support

TuneNow (Chinese name: 聽一下電台) is an iOS and iPadOS internet radio directory and player.

For support, privacy questions, or station and icon removal requests, email:

[cocoaswifty@gmail.com](mailto:cocoaswifty@gmail.com)

## Common Questions

### Why does a station fail to play?

TuneNow plays streams operated by independent broadcasters. A station may be offline, temporarily overloaded, restricted in your region, or using an outdated stream URL.

Check your internet connection, tap the station again, or try another station. For directory-loading errors, use the Retry button shown in the app. Because broadcasters control their own streams, TuneNow cannot guarantee that every listed station is always available.

### Why does TuneNow sometimes use an HTTP stream?

TuneNow's API, search, metadata, and station-icon requests use HTTPS. Some independent broadcasters still provide audio only over HTTP, so a station you choose may connect through an unencrypted stream. TuneNow never sends account credentials, favorites, contacts, or precise location through the audio stream.

See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/TuneNow/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}) for details.

### Why is the current program or track title missing?

Current-title information comes from metadata embedded in the broadcaster's stream. Many stations do not provide it, and TuneNow cannot reconstruct missing or incomplete metadata.

TuneNow does not query Apple iTunes Search or display third-party album artwork.

### Why is a station icon missing?

TuneNow uses only the HTTPS icon URL supplied by Radio Browser. If that URL is missing, unsupported, or unavailable, the app displays a local text placeholder. TuneNow does not scrape broadcaster websites for replacement images.

### Why is a country, category, or search result empty?

Station coverage comes from the community-operated Radio Browser directory. A country or category can appear even when it currently has no working stations, and search results depend on the names and tags supplied by the directory.

Try another search phrase, category, or country, then use Retry if a network error appears.

### Where are favorites and recent stations stored?

Favorites, recent stations, and selected countries are stored locally on your device. TuneNow does not provide an account or cloud sync. You can remove individual favorites in the app; uninstalling the app removes its local container, subject to Apple's backup and restoration behavior.

### Does TuneNow support iPad?

Yes. TuneNow includes a dedicated iPad sidebar and multi-column layout, with portrait and landscape support.

### How do I report an incorrect station or request removal?

Email the support address above and include the station name, country, and relevant stream or icon URL. For a rights or trademark request, state your relationship to the station and identify the material at issue. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the request.

### What should I include in a technical support request?

Please include:

- Device model
- iOS or iPadOS version
- TuneNow app version
- Country and station name involved
- Whether the issue affects browsing, search, station icons, or playback
- A short description of what happened and what you expected

Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

# 聽一下電台支援

TuneNow（中文名稱：聽一下電台）是 iOS 與 iPadOS 網路電台目錄及播放器。

如需技術支援、詢問隱私問題，或申請移除電台／圖示，請寄信至：

[cocoaswifty@gmail.com](mailto:cocoaswifty@gmail.com)

## 常見問題

### 為什麼電台無法播放？

TuneNow 播放由獨立廣播業者營運的串流。電台可能離線、暫時負載過高、限制特定地區，或使用已失效的串流網址。

請先檢查網路後再次點選電台，或改試其他電台。若目錄載入失敗，可使用 App 畫面中的「重試」按鈕。串流由廣播業者自行控制，因此 TuneNow 無法保證所有列出的電台隨時都可播放。

### 為什麼 TuneNow 有時會使用 HTTP 串流？

TuneNow 的 API、搜尋、metadata 與電台圖示請求皆使用 HTTPS。部分獨立廣播業者仍只提供 HTTP 音訊，因此你選擇的電台可能透過未加密串流連線。TuneNow 不會透過音訊串流傳送帳號憑證、收藏、聯絡人或精確位置。

詳細資訊請參閱[隱私權政策]({{ '/TuneNow/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }})。

### 為什麼沒有顯示目前節目或歌曲名稱？

目前標題來自廣播業者嵌入串流的 metadata。許多電台並未提供這項資訊，TuneNow 也無法重建缺少或不完整的 metadata。

TuneNow 不會查詢 Apple iTunes Search，也不會顯示第三方專輯封面。

### 為什麼沒有電台圖示？

TuneNow 只使用 Radio Browser 提供的 HTTPS 圖示網址。若網址不存在、不支援或無法連線，App 會顯示本機文字預留圖。TuneNow 不會爬取廣播業者網站尋找替代圖片。

### 為什麼國家、分類或搜尋結果是空的？

電台資料來自社群營運的 Radio Browser 目錄。即使某個國家或分類目前沒有可用電台，它仍可能顯示；搜尋結果則取決於目錄提供的電台名稱與標籤。

請嘗試其他搜尋詞、分類或國家；若畫面出現網路錯誤，可點選「重試」。

### 收藏與近期電台儲存在哪裡？

收藏、近期電台及選取國家只儲存在你的裝置上。TuneNow 不提供帳號或雲端同步。你可以在 App 內逐一移除收藏；解除安裝 App 會移除其本機 container，但仍受 Apple 備份及回復機制影響。

### TuneNow 支援 iPad 嗎？

支援。TuneNow 為 iPad 提供專用 sidebar 與多欄 layout，並支援直向及橫向操作。

### 如何回報錯誤電台或申請移除內容？

請寄信至上述支援信箱，並附上電台名稱、國家及相關串流或圖示網址。若是權利或商標請求，請說明你與該電台的關係，並指出有疑義的內容。除非處理請求所必需，請勿寄送敏感個人資料。

### 技術支援信件應包含哪些資訊？

請提供：

- 裝置型號
- iOS 或 iPadOS 版本
- TuneNow App 版本
- 相關國家及電台名稱
- 問題發生在瀏覽、搜尋、電台圖示或播放功能
- 實際發生情況與預期結果的簡短說明

請勿提供密碼、付款卡資料或其他敏感資訊。
