# TuneNow Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

TuneNow is an iOS internet radio app. It helps people browse, search, save, and play radio stations from around the world.

This policy explains what information TuneNow stores, what third-party services it contacts, and what the app does not collect.

## Information Stored on Your Device

TuneNow stores the following information locally on your device using Apple UserDefaults:

- Selected country
- Recently selected countries
- Favorite radio stations
- Recently played radio stations

This information stays on your device. TuneNow does not run its own server and does not sync this data to a TuneNow account.

## Third-Party Services

TuneNow uses third-party services to provide app functionality:

- Radio Browser API: radio station lists, countries, categories, stream URLs, station icons, and station metadata
- Apple iTunes Search API: album artwork lookup when a station provides current song information
- Radio station stream providers: audio playback and, when available, stream metadata such as current song title

When the app contacts these services, standard network information such as your IP address may be processed by those third-party services as part of normal internet communication.

## What TuneNow Does Not Do

TuneNow does not:

- Require an account or login
- Sell personal data
- Use advertising SDKs
- Track you across apps or websites
- Access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location
- Store your listening history on a TuneNow server

## App Store Privacy Disclosure

TuneNow uses local storage only for app functionality. The app does not use this information for tracking or advertising.

Radio station availability, stream reliability, song metadata, and album artwork depend on third-party radio data and broadcasters.

This privacy policy may be updated when TuneNow changes its features, data practices, or third-party services. Updates will be posted on this page.

# 聽一下電台隱私權政策摘要
