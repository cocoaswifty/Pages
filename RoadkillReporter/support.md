# RoadkillReporter Support

RoadkillReporter (路殺社回報) is an iOS app for preparing wildlife road mortality reports, keeping local drafts, exploring public records, and submitting confirmed reports to the Taiwan Roadkill Observation Network (TaiRON).

For technical support, privacy questions, or questions about the app's local data, email [cocoaswifty@gmail.com](mailto:cocoaswifty@gmail.com).

## What to include in a support request

Please include:

- Device model and iOS version
- RoadkillReporter app version
- The area involved: sign-in, photos, date or location, drafts, public exploration, website records, or submission
- What happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to the problem
- The exact error message, if one appears

Screenshots can help. Do not include your TaiRON password, session cookies, API keys, or unnecessary personal information in a support email.

## Common questions

### How do I sign in?

RoadkillReporter signs in with an existing TaiRON username or email address and password. The app does not provide native Google or LINE sign-in. Account creation, account management, and website account issues are handled by the TaiRON website.

### Are drafts uploaded automatically?

No. Report drafts and their photos are kept on the device while you prepare them. Upload begins only after you review the confirmation screen and choose the final submission action.

### Why did adding a photo not change my date or location?

When you enter a date or location manually, RoadkillReporter preserves your value. Photo capture time and GPS metadata may help fill a field that has not been set manually. Review the date, time, and location before submission.

### A photo will not load. What should I do?

Confirm that the photo is still available in your Photos library and that RoadkillReporter has permission to access the selected photo. Try choosing the photo again. If the problem continues, include the device model, iOS version, app version, and the error shown; do not email the photo unless support specifically asks for it.

### Where are my drafts and photos stored?

They are stored in the app's local storage on your device. You can continue a draft later or remove drafts from the app. Clearing app data removes local drafts and photos but does not delete an account or a report already accepted by the TaiRON website.

### Why can I not see my website records?

Website records require a successful TaiRON sign-in and an internet connection. RoadkillReporter reads the website's records and keeps a local snapshot for later viewing. The website remains the source of truth for published records and processing results.

### How do I report a problem with a website account or an existing report?

RoadkillReporter cannot change a TaiRON account or delete a report already accepted by the website. Contact the TaiRON website operator through the contact method published in the [TaiRON privacy policy](https://www.roadkill.tw/about/privacy).

# 路殺社回報支援

RoadkillReporter（路殺社回報）是一款 iOS App，協助整理路死動物觀察、保存本機草稿、探索公開資料，並將確認後的回報送至臺灣動物路死觀察網（TaiRON）。

如需技術支援、詢問隱私，或詢問 App 本機資料，請寄信至 [cocoaswifty@gmail.com](mailto:cocoaswifty@gmail.com)。

## 支援信件請提供

請附上：

- 裝置型號與 iOS 版本
- RoadkillReporter App 版本
- 問題發生在哪個功能：登入、照片、日期或位置、草稿、公開探索、網站紀錄，或回報送出
- 實際發生情況、預期結果，以及重現步驟
- 畫面顯示的完整錯誤訊息

如果方便，請附上畫面截圖。請勿在支援信件中提供 TaiRON 密碼、session Cookie、API 金鑰，或無關的個人資料。

## 常見問題

### 如何登入？

RoadkillReporter 使用既有 TaiRON 帳號或電子郵件及密碼登入。App 不提供原生 Google 或 LINE 登入。帳號建立、帳號管理與官網帳號問題，請依 TaiRON 官網處理。

### 草稿會自動上傳嗎？

不會。填寫期間的草稿與照片保存在裝置本機；只有檢查確認頁後，明確選擇最後送出動作，才會開始上傳。

### 為什麼追加照片沒有改變我手動填的日期或位置？

當你已手動填寫日期或位置時，RoadkillReporter 會保留你的內容。照片的拍攝時間與 GPS 中繼資料，只會協助填入尚未手動設定的欄位。送出前請再次核對日期、時間與位置。

### 照片載入失敗怎麼辦？

請確認照片仍在相簿中，並確認 RoadkillReporter 有權限讀取你選取的照片，再重新選取一次。若問題持續，請提供裝置型號、iOS 版本、App 版本及畫面上的錯誤訊息；除非支援人員另行要求，請不要直接寄送照片。

### 草稿與照片儲存在哪裡？

它們儲存在裝置上的 App 本機資料中。你可以稍後繼續草稿，也可以從 App 刪除草稿。清除 App 資料會移除本機草稿與照片，但不會刪除帳號，也不會刪除已被 TaiRON 官網接收的回報。

### 為什麼看不到網站紀錄？

網站紀錄需要成功登入 TaiRON 並且有網路連線。RoadkillReporter 會讀取官網紀錄，並保存本機快取供稍後查看；已公開的紀錄與處理結果仍以官網為準。

### 如何處理官網帳號或既有回報的問題？

RoadkillReporter 無法修改 TaiRON 帳號，也無法刪除已被官網接收的回報。請依[臺灣動物路死觀察網隱私政策](https://www.roadkill.tw/about/privacy)所列的方式聯絡官網營運者。

## 隱私權

請閱讀[RoadkillReporter 隱私權政策]({{ '/RoadkillReporter/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }})，了解本機草稿、照片、位置中繼資料、登入、網站紀錄與回報送出的資料處理方式。
