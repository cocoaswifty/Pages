# RoadkillReporter Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

RoadkillReporter (路殺社回報) is an iOS app that helps people prepare roadkill observation reports, save them as local drafts, and submit confirmed reports to the Taiwan Roadkill Observation Network (TaiRON) website at [roadkill.tw](https://www.roadkill.tw/). This policy covers the app. The TaiRON website, its accounts, its public records, and its own cookies, logs, analytics, and retention practices are operated under the website's own policies.

## Information Stored on Your Device

RoadkillReporter may store the following information in its app container:

- Report drafts, including animal and environment photos, species or taxon selections, notes, specimen status, quantity, date and time, coordinates, location accuracy and source, landmark, road, mileage, and cause of death
- Upload-queue entries, submission status, error messages, and the local state used to prevent an interrupted report from being submitted again without review
- Your TaiRON account email or username and the display name returned by the website
- A local snapshot of your website records, including occurrence number, species, date, location, recorder, identification status, and last-changed time
- Temporary website session cookies used to keep the app's authenticated requests and website pages working

Draft metadata, queue state, account information, and record snapshots are stored locally using the app's storage. Photo files are stored in the app's local Application Support area; queued work may also contain a local copy of the photo data. RoadkillReporter does not store the password you enter for TaiRON.

## Camera, Photos, and Location

The app accesses the camera only when you choose to take a photo. It accesses your photo library only when you choose photos for a report. On the device, the app may read a selected photo's capture time and GPS metadata to help fill in a draft. Reading this metadata does not by itself send the photo or location to the developer.

When you grant When In Use location permission, RoadkillReporter can use a recent device location while you prepare a report. You may also enter coordinates manually. The app does not use location for background tracking. If you choose the final submission action, the confirmed report may include the exact coordinates, location accuracy, date and time, and the photos you selected or captured. Photos may contain embedded metadata, so review them before submitting.

## TaiRON Website, Public Data, and Submission

RoadkillReporter connects directly to roadkill.tw over HTTPS for the following app functions:

- Login with an existing TaiRON account. Your account name or email and password are sent to the website for authentication. The app does not send your password to a RoadkillReporter server or save it after login.
- Read-only public exploration, taxon suggestions, and map or record queries. The selected filters and ordinary network information, such as IP address and request time, may be processed by the website or its hosting providers.
- Account summaries and your website records after login. The app caches a local copy so the records can remain visible if a later read-only sync fails.
- Report submission. The app starts uploading only after you review the confirmation screen and explicitly choose the final submission action. The website may receive the report photos, exact or manually entered location, date and time, taxon and species values, quantity, road and landmark information, cause of death, specimen status, mileage, and notes that you choose to submit.

Some features show roadkill.tw pages inside an embedded web view. Those pages may set cookies or load analytics and other third-party resources under the website operator's control. RoadkillReporter does not control or independently verify the website's retention, analytics, sharing, or deletion practices. For those practices, read the [TaiRON website privacy policy](https://www.roadkill.tw/about/privacy).

## What RoadkillReporter Does Not Do

RoadkillReporter does not:

- Sell personal data
- Use advertising SDKs or a separate third-party analytics SDK
- Use the data listed above for cross-app or cross-website tracking
- Collect or store your TaiRON password after authentication
- Access your contacts, microphone, health data, or biometric data
- Use location in the background

The app can access the camera and photos because those are optional report features. Declining either permission leaves the corresponding feature unavailable but does not prevent you from using other local features.

## Purpose and Retention

Local drafts, photos, queued report data, account display information, and website record snapshots remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear local data and log out, remove the app, or the operating system removes the app's data. iOS backups may preserve app data according to Apple's backup and restore behavior.

Logging out removes the locally saved account information and website session data but can leave local drafts if you choose to keep them. The in-app clear-data action removes local drafts, photo files, upload-queue entries, and cached website records. Clearing local data does not delete a TaiRON account, an already submitted website report, or photos already sent to the website.

After a report is submitted, the TaiRON website operator controls the report's publication, retention, correction, and deletion. Public records may remain public because they support the observation database. RoadkillReporter cannot promise deletion of data that has already been accepted by the website.

## Your Choices and Data Requests

You can:

- Decline camera, photo-library, or location permission
- Use manual coordinates instead of device location
- Keep a report as a local draft and avoid final submission
- Delete drafts and local photos individually, or clear report-related local app data from Settings
- Log out while choosing whether to keep local drafts

For questions about RoadkillReporter or a request concerning data held by the app, contact [cocoaswifty@gmail.com](mailto:cocoaswifty@gmail.com?subject=RoadkillReporter%20privacy%20request). Please do not send a password or session cookie. Requests concerning an existing TaiRON account or a report already submitted to roadkill.tw must also be directed to the website operator through the contact method published in the [TaiRON website privacy policy](https://www.roadkill.tw/about/privacy).

## App Store Privacy Disclosure

For the current app implementation, RoadkillReporter declares the following data categories for app functionality: Email Address, User ID, Photos or Videos, Precise Location, and Other User Content. These categories are linked to the account or report when the relevant feature requires it and are not used for tracking.

The app's privacy manifest also declares its use of UserDefaults as a required-reason API. The App Store privacy answers must be kept consistent with the final app build and with any processing performed by the TaiRON website or embedded website pages.

## This Policy Website

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit it in a browser, GitHub may process standard network and usage information under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

RoadkillReporter is not directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when RoadkillReporter changes its features, data practices, website integrations, or legal obligations. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.

# RoadkillReporter 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 8 日

RoadkillReporter（路殺社回報）是一款 iOS App，協助使用者整理路死動物觀察、保存本機草稿，並將確認後的回報送至[臺灣動物路死觀察網（TaiRON）](https://www.roadkill.tw/)。本政策說明 App 本身的資料處理；TaiRON 官網、官網帳號、公開資料，以及官網自行使用的 Cookie、伺服器紀錄、分析與保存規則，依官網自己的政策處理。

## 儲存在裝置上的資料

RoadkillReporter 可能在 App 容器內保存：

- 回報草稿，包括動物照與環境照、物種或類群選擇、備註、標本狀態、數量、日期時間、座標、定位精度與來源、地標、道路、里程及死因
- 上傳佇列、送出狀態、錯誤訊息，以及避免中斷後未經確認再次送出回報的本機狀態
- TaiRON 帳號的電子郵件或帳號名稱，以及官網回傳的顯示名稱
- 官網紀錄的本機快取，包括回報編號、物種、日期、地點、紀錄者、鑑定狀態與最後修改時間
- 讓 App 的登入請求與官網頁面正常運作所需的暫時性網站 session Cookie

草稿中繼資料、佇列狀態、帳號資訊與紀錄快取會使用 App 本機儲存。照片檔案存放在 App 的本機 Application Support 目錄；待處理的佇列工作也可能在本機保存一份照片資料。RoadkillReporter 不會保存你輸入的 TaiRON 密碼。

## 相機、照片與位置

只有在你選擇拍照時，App 才會存取相機；只有在你選擇照片加入回報時，App 才會存取相簿。App 可能在裝置上讀取你選取照片的拍攝時間與 GPS 中繼資料，以協助填寫草稿。讀取這些中繼資料本身不會將照片或位置傳送給開發者。

在你授予「使用 App 期間」定位權限後，RoadkillReporter 可以在準備回報時使用近期裝置位置；你也可以手動輸入座標。App 不會在背景追蹤位置。若你選擇最後的送出動作，確認後的回報可能包含精確座標、定位精度、日期時間，以及你選取或拍攝的照片。照片可能帶有嵌入式中繼資料，請在送出前檢查。

## TaiRON 官網、公開資料與回報送出

RoadkillReporter 會透過 HTTPS 直接連線到 roadkill.tw，提供以下功能：

- 使用既有 TaiRON 帳號登入。帳號名稱或電子郵件及密碼會送至官網驗證；App 不會將密碼送到 RoadkillReporter 的伺服器，也不會在登入後保存密碼。
- 讀取公開探索資料、物種建議及地圖或紀錄查詢。你選擇的篩選條件，以及 IP 位址、請求時間等一般網路資訊，可能由官網或其託管服務處理。
- 登入後讀取帳號摘要與官網紀錄。App 會保存本機快取，讓稍後的唯讀同步失敗時仍能顯示上一份紀錄。
- 送出回報。只有在你檢視確認頁並明確選擇最後送出動作後，App 才會開始上傳。官網可能收到你選擇送出的回報照片、精確或手動輸入的位置、日期時間、類群與物種、數量、道路與地標、死因、標本狀態、里程及備註。

部分功能會在內嵌網頁中顯示 roadkill.tw 頁面。這些頁面可能依官網營運者的控制設定 Cookie，或載入分析與其他第三方資源。RoadkillReporter 不控制，也無法獨立驗證官網的保存、分析、分享或刪除方式；相關資訊請閱讀[臺灣動物路死觀察網隱私政策](https://www.roadkill.tw/about/privacy)。

## RoadkillReporter 不會進行的行為

RoadkillReporter 不會：

- 出售個人資料
- 使用廣告 SDK 或另外加入第三方分析 SDK
- 將上述資料用於跨 App 或跨網站追蹤
- 在驗證後收集或保存 TaiRON 密碼
- 存取聯絡人、麥克風、健康資料或生物辨識資料
- 在背景使用位置

App 會因為選用的回報功能而存取相機與照片。拒絕任一權限只會停用相應功能，不會阻止其他本機功能使用。

## 使用目的與保存期限

本機草稿、照片、待送出回報資料、帳號顯示資訊與官網紀錄快取會留在裝置上，直到你在 App 內刪除、清除本機資料並登出、移除 App，或作業系統移除 App 資料為止。iOS 備份可能依 Apple 的備份與回復機制保留 App 資料。

登出會移除本機保存的帳號資訊與網站 session 資料；如果你選擇保留，則本機草稿仍會留下。App 內的清除資料功能會移除本機草稿、照片檔案、上傳佇列與官網紀錄快取。清除本機資料不會刪除 TaiRON 帳號、已送出的官網回報，或已傳送給官網的照片。

回報送出後，回報的公開、保存、修正與刪除由 TaiRON 官網營運者決定。公開紀錄可能因為支援觀察資料庫而持續公開。RoadkillReporter 無法承諾刪除已被官網接收的資料。

## 你的選擇與資料請求

你可以：

- 拒絕相機、相簿或定位權限
- 使用手動座標，不使用裝置定位
- 將回報留在本機草稿，不執行最後送出
- 個別刪除草稿與本機照片，或從「設定」清除回報相關的本機 App 資料
- 登出時選擇是否保留本機草稿

若對 RoadkillReporter 有疑問，或要提出與 App 保存資料有關的請求，請聯絡 [cocoaswifty@gmail.com](mailto:cocoaswifty@gmail.com?subject=RoadkillReporter%20privacy%20request)。請勿寄送密碼或 session Cookie。若請求涉及既有 TaiRON 帳號，或已送至 roadkill.tw 的回報，也必須透過[臺灣動物路死觀察網隱私政策](https://www.roadkill.tw/about/privacy)所列的聯絡方式向官網營運者提出。

## App Store 隱私揭露

依目前 App 實作，RoadkillReporter 為 App 功能宣告以下資料類別：電子郵件地址、使用者 ID、照片或影片、精確位置，以及其他使用者內容。相關功能需要時，這些資料會與帳號或回報連結，且不會用於追蹤。

App 的 privacy manifest 也宣告為 UserDefaults 使用必要理由 API。App Store 的隱私問卷必須與最終 App build，以及 TaiRON 官網或內嵌網頁實際進行的資料處理保持一致。

## 政策變更

當 RoadkillReporter 的功能、資料處理方式、網站整合或法律義務變更時，本政策可能更新；更新後的生效日期會顯示在本頁上方。
