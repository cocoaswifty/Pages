# FreshRadar Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

FreshRadar is an iOS app for finding discounted fresh food at nearby convenience stores in Taiwan. It helps people search nearby FamilyMart and 7-ELEVEN stores, view available discounted products, save favorite stores, and create local product tracking alerts.

FreshRadar is not operated by FamilyMart, 7-ELEVEN, OpenPoint, or Google.

This policy explains what information FreshRadar stores, what third-party services it contacts, and what the app does not collect.

## Information Stored on Your Device

FreshRadar stores the following information locally on your device:

- App theme preference
- Notification preference
- Favorite stores
- Product tracking list, including tracked store names and product keywords
- Product IDs already seen for tracking notifications
- Product catalog cache and catalog version
- Product image cache

Theme, notification preference, favorites, tracking data, and catalog version are stored using Apple UserDefaults. Product catalog cache files and product image cache files are stored in the app's local storage or cache directory.

This information stays on your device. FreshRadar does not run its own account system and does not sync this data to a FreshRadar server.

## Location

FreshRadar asks for location permission so it can search for nearby convenience stores and calculate distance from your current location.

When you search nearby stores, the app sends latitude and longitude coordinates to the store data services needed to return nearby store and product availability results. If no nearby discounted products are found, the app may search a small surrounding area to improve results.

FreshRadar does not use location for advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale of personal data.

## Notifications

FreshRadar can send local notifications when a tracked store has matching discounted products. Notification settings and tracking keywords are stored locally on your device.

FreshRadar does not send your tracking list to a FreshRadar server.

## Third-Party Services

FreshRadar contacts third-party services to provide app functionality:

- FamilyMart services: nearby store and discounted product availability
- 7-ELEVEN / OpenPoint LoveFood services: nearby store and discounted product availability
- Google Apps Script endpoint maintained for FreshRadar: product catalog metadata used to supplement product names, images, prices, categories, or calories
- Product image hosts: product images shown in the app
- Apple Maps: directions when you choose to open a store in Maps

When the app contacts these services, standard network information such as your IP address may be processed by those services as part of normal internet communication. Location coordinates may be sent when needed to search nearby stores.

## What FreshRadar Does Not Do

FreshRadar does not:

- Require an account or login
- Sell personal data
- Use advertising SDKs
- Track you across apps or websites
- Access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
- Store your precise location on a FreshRadar server
- Share your favorites or tracking keywords with a FreshRadar server

## App Store Privacy Disclosure

FreshRadar uses precise location for app functionality when you grant location permission. The app does not use location for tracking or advertising.

FreshRadar uses local storage for app functionality and declares UserDefaults usage in its privacy manifest.

This privacy policy may be updated when FreshRadar changes its features, data practices, or third-party services. Updates will be posted on this page.

# 鮮食雷達隱私權政策摘要

生效日期：2026 年 5 月 24 日

鮮食雷達是用來查詢台灣附近便利商店即期折扣鮮食的 iOS App。App 可搜尋附近全家與 7-ELEVEN 門市、查看折扣商品、收藏店家，並建立本機商品追蹤通知。

鮮食雷達不是由全家、7-ELEVEN、OpenPoint 或 Google 營運。

App 會在裝置本機保存主題設定、通知設定、收藏店家、追蹤店家與商品關鍵字、已看過的商品 ID、商品目錄快取與商品圖片快取。這些資料不會同步到鮮食雷達自有伺服器。

當你允許位置權限並搜尋附近店家時，App 會使用目前位置計算距離，並將經緯度傳送給必要的全家或 7-ELEVEN / OpenPoint LoveFood 服務，以取得附近門市與折扣商品資料。若附近沒有結果，App 可能會查詢周邊小範圍座標來改善搜尋結果。

App 也會連線到鮮食雷達維護的 Google Apps Script 商品目錄來源，以補足商品圖片、價格、分類或熱量等資料；商品圖片會從對應圖片來源載入。當你選擇開啟導航時，App 會使用 Apple Maps。

鮮食雷達不需要帳號登入、不使用廣告 SDK、不追蹤使用者跨 App 或網站的活動、不出售個人資料，也不會存取聯絡人、照片、麥克風或相機。
