# QTap Support

Need help with QTap? Contact support:

cocoaswifty@gmail.com

Please include:

- Your device model and iOS version
- QTap app version
- Whether notification permission is enabled and QTap shows the channel as ready
- A short description of what happened and what you expected

Do not include your channel sender URL, device credential, or APNs token. Anyone with a sender URL can send notifications to that channel.

# QTap 支援

需要 QTap 使用協助，請聯絡：

cocoaswifty@gmail.com

來信請附上：

- 裝置型號與 iOS 版本
- QTap App 版本
- 是否已允許通知，以及 QTap 是否顯示頻道已就緒
- 問題發生情況與你預期的結果

請勿附上頻道推播網址、裝置憑證或 APNs token。任何取得推播網址的人都能向該頻道發送通知。
